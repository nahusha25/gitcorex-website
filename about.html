Who We Are

Company Overview

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, gitCoreX is an information technology enterprise offering website development, custom web applications, digital engineering, and enterprise resource planning (ERP) solutions.

Modern organizations face a dual challenge: they need an authoritative, high-performance web presence to engage customers, while simultaneously requiring streamlined internal systems to manage inventory, sales, procurement, accounting, and workflows. gitCoreX addresses both dimensions through disciplined architecture, clean code standards, and transparent milestone delivery.

We do not force businesses into rigid, generic templates. Every website and ERP configuration is engineered around genuine operational requirements, ensuring our software directly enhances productivity and long-term organizational value.
