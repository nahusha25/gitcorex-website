Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • gitCoreX (GSTIN: 29FBAPR5805L1Z5)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how gitCoreX ("we", "our", or "us"), headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, collects, processes, and protects information submitted by visitors to our official website (gitCoreX) when requesting consultations, submitting inquiries, or applying for technical employment.

We respect your privacy and are committed to treating personal and business information with strict confidentiality and in compliance with applicable Indian data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly when intentionally submitted by visitors through our website forms:

A. Contact Form Data Collected

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact & Consultation form, we collect the following data fields:

  • name: Full name of the individual or organization contact
  • phone: 10-digit telephone contact number
  • email: Email address for project communications and consultation replies
  • service: Service or solution area of interest (e.g. Website Development, Custom Web App, ERP Solutions, E-Commerce, Maintenance & Support, General Inquiry)
  • budget: Estimated budget range for the requested project
  • timeline: Intended delivery timeline or deadline
  • message: Project specifications, business requirements, or inquiry description

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered directly to gitCoreX by email at gitcorex26@gmail.com.

B. Careers Form Data Collected

When you apply for a career opening through our Careers page, we collect the following data fields:

  • name: Full legal name of the candidate
  • phone: 10-digit contact phone number
  • email: Personal or professional email address
  • role: Technical position applied for (e.g. Web Developer, ERP Developer / Specialist, UI/UX Designer, Mobile App Developer, Digital Solutions Specialist)
  • experience: Number of years of professional technology experience
  • details: Summary of technical skills, background, and technology stacks

Careers form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to us by email. Candidate resumes are received directly by email at gitcorex26@gmail.com rather than stored on web servers.

3. Why Information is Collected & Purpose of Use

Data is collected exclusively to communicate with you and evaluate your submission, and it is used only to respond to enquiries and applications:

  • Contact Form Data (name, phone, email, service, budget, timeline, message): Why it is collected: To evaluate your project scope and technical requirements, provide customized technical consultations and quotes, and respond to your enquiry within our 24-hour response service commitment. Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to us by email. It is used only to respond to your enquiries.
  • Careers Form Data (name, phone, email, role, experience, resume): Why it is collected: To evaluate your technical capabilities and experience, review your qualifications against open company requirements, and communicate regarding recruitment interview stages. Careers form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to us by email, and candidate resumes are received by email at gitcorex26@gmail.com. It is used only to respond to your employment applications.
  • Strict Non-Marketing Guarantee: We do NOT sell, lease, rent, monetize, or disclose your contact information to third-party marketing companies, data brokers, or advertising networks. We will never use your contact details to send unsolicited promotional spam.
  • Email-Based Resume Handling: Candidate resumes are received directly by email at gitcorex26@gmail.com. We do not host or store applicant resumes on public web servers, ensuring maximum confidentiality and security for all candidates.

4. Information Security & Non-Disclosure

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact details to third-party marketing companies. Form submissions are processed securely by Web3Forms and delivered directly to our official company email inbox (gitcorex26@gmail.com). Resumes are received and managed exclusively by email, and data is accessible only by authorized team members evaluating project specifications or job applications.

5. Data Retention

We retain client communication records only for as long as necessary to fulfill project evaluation, contract execution, legal obligations, or until a client requests deletion of their inquiry records.
